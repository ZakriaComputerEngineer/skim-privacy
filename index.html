Skim Browser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Skim Browser ("Skim") is a web browser designed to protect your privacy. This policy explains what data Skim handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Skim has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not collect, receive or sell any information about you.
  • Everything Skim stores — history, bookmarks, settings, downloads list, site permissions and privacy statistics — stays on your device.
  • Skim only connects to the internet to load the websites you visit and the few services listed below.

Private windows keep their cookies, history and site data in memory only, and discard them when the window closes. You can also have Skim clear cookies, history and cache every time it closes (Settings → Shields & privacy).

Network connections Skim makes

  • Websites you visit, as with any browser.
  • Search suggestions (optional): while you type in the address bar, what you type is sent to your chosen search engine (DuckDuckGo by default) to show suggestions. Turn this off in Settings → Search. These requests don't include your cookies.
  • Filter-list updates: Skim periodically downloads public ad- and tracker-blocking lists (from Ghostery's servers, which host EasyList, EasyPrivacy and similar lists). These downloads don't include any personal information.

Skim sends the Global Privacy Control signal to websites by default, asking them not to sell or share your data.

Websites and third parties

Websites you visit have their own privacy policies and may collect information about you. Skim's Shields block many trackers and third-party cookies, but no browser can block all tracking. Your chosen search engine handles your searches under its own policy.

Children

Skim is a general-purpose browser and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
